# Design principles

Where these come from: a reference sample of **43 live Яндекс.Кит storefronts** that designers picked as well-made: their page layouts, native section settings and themes, and full-page screenshots of home pages and product pages (desktop and phone) and of category pages (desktop). Counts read "N of 43 stores" unless they count section instances; "N of 40" and "N of 41" count only the stores whose home page (40) or storefront (41) carries the native widget measured, the rest use a custom section or none. The sample judges whole stores, not single sections, so frequency stands in for good practice, except where the majority is a failure (text baked into images, dead tiles, placeholders): there the principle goes against the sample and says so. Where a principle rests on few stores, it says so.

Two cautions about the counts:

- **Template copies inflate them.** Several stores in the sample were built from the same starter or by the same designer, and share settings in groups of about 5 to 11 stores: 42 native category listings come from about 17 independent decisions, the product cards of 40 stores from about 22. So «in 6 stores» may be one decision made six times. Where it matters, the evidence says so; weigh a count by the independent designs behind it, not by the stores.
- **The sample leans to fashion and beauty.** About half of it is apparel and accessories, most of the rest cosmetics, health and home objects; food, pets, electronics, auto parts and service businesses are absent or too rare to count (a store or two at most). For those niches start from `home-other-niche` and the universal recipes, and treat niche-specific advice here as fashion advice.

Each principle: what to do, how to do it in Кит, the evidence, and the `plan lint` rule that checks it (if a machine can). The mobile principles live in [`mobile.md`](mobile.md), the product page in [`product-page.md`](product-page.md), category and catalog pages in [`listing.md`](listing.md).

## Never invent

| Value | Where it comes from | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|
| Category and collection `slug` (shelf `dataSource`, tile and banner links) | The pulled workspace first. A shelf's `dataSource` is exactly `type`, `slug` and `id`, all required: the storefront fetches by `type` + `slug`, and `section add` refuses a new shelf without `id`. For a category or collection `id` is the cabinet's id of it (checked on a live store); live shelves mostly carry none, or one platform-wide value, and some a leftover `slugId`: keep a pulled shelf's own values, write the three fields for a new one. Tile and banner links: existing links. The constructor cannot look categories up; for a category or collection the workspace does not show, **yandex-kit-cabinet** lists them read-only (`list /v1/categories`, `list /v1/collections`: `id`, `title`, `slug`, `status`). | A `relevant` or `last_seen` shelf needs no catalog id: `id` and `slug` are the type itself (checked on a live store). Otherwise ask the merchant or leave the tile unlinked and say so. |
| The mapping from cabinet ids to section fields | Live sections suggest it: a category tile links to `/catalog/<slug>`, a collection tile to `/collections/<slug>`, and wherever the sample has both, a tile's `linkId` equals the `slugId` some older shelves keep for the same category or collection (a handful of cases). A shelf's `dataSource.id` takes the cabinet's `id` (checked on a live store); that a tile's `linkId` is the same id is likely but **not verified**: write the cabinet `id` as `linkId` and check the tile on the proposal preview. | Leave the block unlinked or the shelf out and say why. |
| A product link (hero button, banner, tile) | The product page address the merchant copies from the storefront, in both `link` and `linkId`, as the sample's product links carry it. **yandex-kit-cabinet** (`list /v1/products`) confirms that the product exists; it gives no page address. Check the link on the proposal preview. | Link to the category instead, or leave the block unlinked and say so. |
| `linkSource.link` + `linkId` | Internal page: its path plus the page, category or collection id from the workspace. External link: the URL in both fields. | Leave both empty and tell the user the block does not click. |
| Images and video | `kit.py … media upload` / `media video-upload` (turn the upload output into an image object with `design.py media-object`), or media already in the workspace. | No block. Never a placeholder or data-URI image. |
| Palette ids other than `default` | `global_settings.appearance.colors.palettes[].id` of this store. | Add the palette with a theme preset first. |
| Menu ids (Header `modules.main.menu.menu`, or `modules.main.menu` in the older flat shape; `modules.additional.menu`; Footer `modules[].menu`, `legalInfo.menu`) | The pulled Header and Footer; menus are managed in the cabinet. | Keep the existing value. Never empty the main menu or a footer menu (an empty main menu leaves phones without a burger). The secondary `modules.additional.menu` may be emptied on purpose: it is valid and removes the sticky second row on phones. |
| `form.promocode.promocodeId` | The merchant's cabinet (yandex-kit-cabinet). | Leave `form.promocode` out and promise no discount. |
| `settings.version` | Never by hand: `section add` stamps it, `section set` keeps it. | — |
| `brandPrimary` | Brand assets or the merchant. | Keep the store's value. |
| Offer terms, delivery threshold, discount, gifts, return window, requisites, privacy and offer texts, FAQ answers, loyalty terms, advantage types on the product card, reviews | The merchant. | Draft the structure, list the questions, wait for answers. |
| Contacts, logo, social links | Cabinet data; sections only switch and size them. | Turn the `show*` switch off when the value is empty or a placeholder. |
| Split, Yandex discount, age gate, out-of-stock consent | Store configuration. | Keep the store's values. |

## The principles

1. **Set the theme first; let sections inherit.** Width, rhythm, gutters, heading alignment and the product card are theme settings; sections write `default` for their own title alignment and full-width flag unless a preset needs a specific look. *Evidence:* 1793 of 2268 sections leave the side paddings unset and inherit them; explicit `left` titles under a centred theme (141 sections in 24 stores) are the most common visual inconsistency. Two titles never follow the theme: a Faq title follows only its own `appearance.align` (`presets apply --work` centres a titled Faq under a centred theme), and the category name over a listing is always on the left. A service or legal page is the exception that stays left as a whole (`service-page-text`, `faq-service-left`). *Lint:* `title-align-mixed`.
2. **Start from what designers choose, not from platform defaults.** See [`theme.md`](theme.md). Check the theme font too: text set in an italic or decorative face is the cabinet's to change, and `presets apply theme` and `plan lint --work` print which font the theme uses. *Evidence:* 34-41 of 43 stores override each of the listed defaults. *Lint:* `theme-font`.
3. **One hero, one slide, right under the header.** Rotation (2-6 slides) only for multi-brand or promo-heavy retail, every slide linked, one text style. The hero's button leads to products: a collection or category page (new arrivals, bestsellers), not /catalog, which shows only category tiles and costs one more click. No second hero-sized banner right under it, a custom hero included: two first screens push the categories and the products off the phone's first two screens. *Evidence:* 33 of 43 home pages open with a banner; 607 of 633 carousel sections have one slide; on a demo store a custom hero over a full-screen banner went unreported. *Lint:* `one-hero`, `hero-first`, `hero-slides`, `hero-stack`.
4. **Every banner has its own mobile image, re-composed for the phone.** The paired ratio is the default: 2:1 > 4:3, 5:2 > 4:3, 7:2 > 7:3, 5:1 > 5:2, 10:1 > 7:1, fit-screen > fit-screen. The content may choose otherwise: a strip with more than about 4 words becomes a 4:3 card, an immersive hero a full-height portrait ([`mobile.md`](mobile.md)). *Evidence:* 670 of 697 slides carry a separate mobile image; designed stores turn text strips into 4:3 cards in several stores and heroes into full-height portraits in 6. *Lint:* `banner-mobile-image`, `banner-ratio-pair`, `banner-mobile-same`.
5. **Live text over text baked into images.** Prefer slide titles, tile titles, RichText and ImageWithText. If text must be in the image: a separate mobile crop, text in the central 80% (carousel arrows cover the edges), never prices or offer terms. `fit-screen` crops, so only with live text or text-free imagery. *Evidence:* only 71 of 697 slides have a live title. This is a UX and search rule that goes against the sample: designers bake text, and it costs search, accessibility, editing and the phone layout, where baked text renders at about half size. Live text lands where the slide's alignment puts it (the left ~40% of a desktop frame for the hero presets): compose the art with that zone empty ([`mobile.md`](mobile.md#where-the-live-text-lands)), and keep a 7:3 or 5:2 phone image to a title of about 5 words. A button on a darkened slide or under white text is `primary`: the default `secondary` reads as switched off there. *Lint:* `button-text`, `button-text-hidden`, `banner-live-text-fit`, `banner-button-contrast`.
6. **Alternate image moments and shopping grids, and make the order the store's own.** Hero > category tiles > shelf > visual break > shelf > story > shelf > closing band is the sample's median, not a template: choose each recipe slot's option by the look ([`looks.md`](looks.md)), vary at least two content slots (not the bar, header or footer) from their first option, mix at least 5 kinds of blocks and give home one signature block. *Evidence:* the most frequent neighbours on home are tiles and shelves (41 times each way) and banner then tiles (39); the median home has 9 content sections: 3 banners, 2 tile blocks, 2 shelves. A live check of four demo stores of different niches built from the median recipes gave one skeleton with the same heading scale in all four. *Lint:* `adjacent-shelves-loose`, `rhythm-monotony`.
7. **No two same-shaped shelves back to back.** The only allowed pair is the bento: a 2-column lead shelf, then an untitled shelf with another column count and `paddingTop: none`. *Evidence:* 9 of 40 stores place shelves next to each other; about 9 of 15 such joints are a deliberate bento, the rest monotone 4 + 4 stacks. *Lint:* `adjacent-shelves`.
8. **One data source per shelf per page:** new, bestsellers, sale, a category; a source is its `type` + `slug`. *Evidence:* 7 of 40 home pages point two shelves at one source and show identical rows. *Lint:* `shelf-source-repeat`, `shelf-no-source`.
9. **Category entry within the first two content sections:** captioned tiles linked to real categories. Their title is a phrase about what the store sells: the storefront makes the first titled section the page's H1, a banner title never is one, nor a shelf title that links to its category or collection (a shelf on another source, recently viewed or relevant, has a plain title that can be), and `seo_h1` is not printed on home or custom pages. *Evidence:* tiles right after the hero in 25 of 43; captioned category tiles in 26 stores; on the live check of demo stores the home H1 fell to a section from the middle of the page. *Lint:* `category-entry`, `page-h1`.
10. **Every navigation tile and every call-to-action banner leads somewhere;** purely decorative image tiles may stay unlinked on purpose. *Evidence:* only 187 of 1055 tiles link anywhere; 51 of 69 mid-page banners are dead ends. A UX rule that goes against the sample: most designed stores break it, so the lint warns and never blocks. The same holds for whole pages: a new visitor's empty cart, the 404 page, the catalog and the blog end with a shelf of products. *Lint:* `tile-links`, `banner-links`, `page-dead-end`.
11. **Media sits flush, commerce carries the air.** Banners, splits and diptychs `paddingTop/Bottom: none` (edge to edge with side padding `0`); shelves `single` or `double`. *Evidence:* on home pages the joints are single-single 123 times, double-single 44, none-single 35, none-none 33. *Lint:* —.
12. **Glue a heading to what it titles:** heading RichText `paddingBottom: none`, the next section `paddingTop: none` and its own title emptied. One heading per block. *Evidence:* 64 heading sections in 11 stores. *Lint:* —.
13. **The body sits on the base palette; colour comes from imagery and one accent.** Spend other palettes on 1-2 bands per page, the closing band and the footer; never alternate palettes section by section. *Evidence:* 1407 of 1634 sections use the base palette. *Lint:* `palette-missing`, `palette-churn`.
14. **Palette bands for chapters.** Consecutive sections on one palette merge into a band (brand story, proof plus subscription); the first section of the band gets `double` on top, the last `double` at the bottom. *Evidence:* the three-palette theme (base, light, dark) is the most common set, 20 of 43. *Lint:* —.
15. **Close with a band.** EmailSubscription is the last content section before the Footer, often on the footer palette; the footer sits on a contrasting palette (dark or tinted). *Evidence:* subscription last on 23 of 43 home pages, sharing the footer palette in 10-11; a contrasting footer in 26 stores (dark about 14, light tint about 12). About 3 in 10 designed homes have no subscription at all, so its absence is a warning. Most of the sample's forms promise a discount in nearly the same words, while the schema's own default subtitle promises none: the wording travels with a template the stores share, not with the platform default (which template, a starter or a seed, is not verified). Whoever writes it, the lint asks for a promocode wherever a discount is promised. *Lint:* `subscribe-missing`, `subscribe-last`, `subscribe-above-fold`, `discount-without-promocode`.
16. **One brand-story block in the middle third that leads further, at a reading width.** The kit-custom-sections pattern `text-media-split` by default (an eyebrow, a heading phrase, 16 px text in a 560-640 px column, a button to a real page, a 1:1 or 3:4 photo); natively ImageWithText `brand-story-split` in a narrowed section of the theme's box (`hPaddingDesktop` 1500 and `isFullscreen: disabled`: a text column of about 520 px, 70 characters a line; under a theme that spans sections across the screen the column runs about 775 px on a 1920 px window; in a theme box of 1728-1920 px (`maxWidthDesktop`) no side padding a section may write brings it under about 660 px, so there keep the copy within 200 characters or take `text-media-split`) with a `###` heading, one paragraph and a last-line link, or a centred statement RichText. Stacked image-and-text blocks alternate sides. ImageWithText splits the section 50/50 and has no button: at the theme width its text column runs 110-120 characters a line and the block ends nowhere. *Evidence:* 9 stores use exactly one ImageWithText in the lower half. About half the home pages in the sample carry no live story block at all (no RichText, ImageWithText or custom section): their story is baked into images. The principle keeps it live. In the live check of demo stores a native split at the theme width gave long lines, a heading cut off from its text and no way on. *Lint:* `zigzag`, `image-sliver`, `long-copy`, `story-dead-end`, `story-measure`.
17. **A proof block for considered purchases:** reviews, press, customer photos, stores or certificates — one or two, real ones, between the last shelves and the closing band. *Evidence:* imagery proof in 3 of 7 stores of one analysed group; not counted across the whole sample. *Lint:* —.
18. **A transparent header only over an edge-to-edge BannersCarousel hero,** dark or mid-tone, with a light inverted logo in the cabinet, and only on a home-only header instance; on the shared header it turns every page transparent, and on a white product page the header vanishes. The header lies over the image only of a BannersCarousel that comes first: over any other first section (a custom hero included) the storefront pulls that section's background up under the header, so the bar sits on the background colour and the image starts below it, and a transparent header always shows the inverted logo, whatever `transparencyColor` says: over a light hero the logo disappears. Every other page's header is opaque (`header-opaque-off-home`). *Evidence:* 10 of 40 home pages; 5 of them also transparent on the product page. *Lint:* `transparent-header`, `transparent-composite`.
19. **Header archetype by the shape of the catalogue:** centred logo + links for brands with a flat menu of up to 6-8 items (`centered-logo-links`), centred logo + burger when the menu is nested or long, left logo + links for short menus, catalog button + wide search for large assortments. On desktop the categories show without a click: links show only the top level and the first 8 items. Always sticky. *Evidence:* centre + burger on 19 of 40 home pages, centre + links in 5 stores; sticky on 41 of 41; burger-only desktop headers drew the critics' complaint in the live check of demo stores, so no recipe's header slot opens with the burger: it is the option for a nested or long menu. *Lint:* `header-burger` (info: the lint cannot see how deep the menu is).
20. **Announcement bar: one short commercial promise** that fits one phone line: 35 characters or less, title and body together, the key number in the first 25; consistent with the delivery page and the subscription offer. No contacts in the bar: phones hide them. *Evidence:* one module in 15 of 21 live bars; phones cut a module at about 33 bold or 47 regular characters, and about 7 in 10 live home bars fit. *Lint:* `announcement-length`, `announcement-contacts`.
21. **One photo ratio, set by the product type:** 3:4 for apparel on models, 1:1 for objects and packshots; tiles, shelves and the listing share it. *Evidence:* 3:4 in 34 stores, 1:1 in 9, the platform's 4:3 in none. *Lint:* —.
22. **A short home:** 8-14 content sections, no more than about 4 shelves, about 10 phone screens or fewer. *Evidence:* median 9 sections, range 4-19; median 9 phone screens. *Lint:* `home-length`, `shelf-count`, `touch-length`.
23. **Zero placeholders at publish.** No template text, no visible placeholder tile titles, no all-zero phone, no empty image or banner slide without one, no subscription discount without a promocode, no service page linked before it has the merchant's text, no empty SEO title or description on a published static page (the storefront fills in a generic title and a description that promises bonuses, instalments and cashback). *Evidence:* a service page left with the template placeholder is still linked from the footer. *Lint:* `placeholder-marker`, `platform-placeholder`, `media-missing`, `discount-without-promocode`, `consent-text`, `service-page-empty`, `faq-empty`, `faq-repeated`, `richtext-empty`, `seo-meta-empty`, `advantages-unconfirmed` (advantages in the platform wording or a template's text), `shelf-seeded-source` (a shelf or link into the template's seeded «Категория 1-го уровня»), `page-empty`, `system-widget-missing`.
24. **Native for commerce, custom for the brand.** Shelves, listings, the product card, the cart, category tiles and the subscription native; the signature block, the story with a button and reviews may be custom — see [`native-vs-custom.md`](native-vs-custom.md). *Evidence:* 36 of 43 home pages are native-only; the other 7 hold 33 custom sections between them. *Lint:* `custom-share` (more than 4 on home), `custom-without-template`.
25. **Long text at a reading measure: 60-80 characters a line.** RichText over about 400 characters at `columns` 6 (about 650 px in a 1400 px box, about 75 characters at 15-16 px), service and legal pages left at 6 (`service-page-text`), questions at 8 (every Faq preset), all of them in the theme's box: the text presets write `section.isFullscreen: disabled`, because under a theme that spans sections across the screen (`isFullscreen: true`, as `layout-editorial` and `layout-soft-friendly` set it) a section left at `default` is as wide as the window and 6 columns run about 930 px on a 1920 px screen. The limit is the line, not the column count: in a theme box of 1728 or 1920 px (`maxWidthDesktop`) 6 columns run 850-950 px, so `presets apply --work` (and the lint, for a plan that names the preset) takes a text preset down to 5 columns there, a Faq to 5-6, and widens the side padding only where 5 columns are still too wide; an article's paragraphs aligned left (Blog `article-left`; `alignContent: default` follows a centred theme and centres them). The platform's RichText default of 8 columns runs about 115 characters, and a Faq at 12 about 1300 px. *Evidence:* not counted on the sample; the widths follow from the grid, and the live check of demo stores found service pages at about 115 characters a line and centred article paragraphs on a centred theme. *Lint:* `text-measure`.

## What the sample does not settle

- Whether a new `relevant` or `last_seen` shelf renders with the type as its `dataSource.id`: of 110 live shelves of these types, 90 carry none and 20 the shared platform value, none the literal; the schema requires one. Check the proposal preview.
- How to create a home-only header instance: some stores run one, the constructor offers no command for it.
- Whether the cabinet's category and collection `id` is what a tile's or banner's `linkId` expects (see «Never invent»; for a shelf's `dataSource.id` it is checked): check each new link on the proposal preview.
- Rendering of Faq `align: two-columns` and `expand: all`: unused in the sample.
- Screenshots exist for home pages (desktop and phone), product pages (desktop and phone) and category pages (desktop only). Category pages on phones, and every other page type, rest on section data.
