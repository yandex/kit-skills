# kit-storefront-design — contract for agents

Before editing this skill, read the repository contract: [`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md).

## Safety contract

1. **Offline for good.** `scripts/` has no `urllib`, no sockets, no `subprocess` and never reads a token (`KIT_TOKEN`, `KIT_TOKEN_FILE`, `~/.yandex-kit-skills/kit_api.token`). Every store read and write is a `kit-storefront-constructor` command the agent runs itself; this skill never runs another skill's script and never adds `--confirm` for it.
2. **A preset carries no store value.** No id, slug, menu id, palette id other than the ones a theme preset creates, media, link target, promocode, text or `settings.version` in `settings` or `module_style`. What a preset cannot supply goes to its `fill` list; applying a preset therefore can never invent anything. Empty strings are allowed only where emptiness is the design (an untitled second shelf, a heading moved into markdown).
3. **Real key names only.** Every preset is validated against storefront BFF schema snapshots with the constructor's own validator, over a skeleton (the `section add` path) and over a sparse pulled section (the `section set` path), plus a check that every key it writes is declared by the schema (the schema does not forbid unknown keys, so a typo would otherwise pass and do nothing). That suite lives in the internal package with the other evals; run it after any preset change.
4. **Evidence is counts, never stores.** Recipes, presets and principles come from a reference sample of 43 live storefronts. Describe what the sample shows as shares of it ("in about 3 in 5 stores", "26 of 43"); never name a store, a brand or a person, never quote their texts, never link their images, never give a count a visitor could observe on one live store (its categories, variants or sections). Where a count was not taken, say so rather than estimate. A preset or recipe that rests on fewer than 3 stores is marked `"idea": true` (`doctor` enforces it for evidence that says so) and is never a default, unless its `basis` names the platform behaviour or the principle it rests on instead (`lead-under-hero`: the storefront's H1). An idea may be a recipe slot's later option or a look's choice, never a slot's first option. Nothing may reproduce one store: a preset whose distinctive values match a single store, or a recipe whose section sequence only one store has, is changed or dropped; the internal suite has the audit that finds them (run it after every change to a preset's settings or a recipe's steps: a single value can turn a preset into one store's fingerprint, and the unit tests do not see the sample).
5. **The lint is advice with teeth.** Errors are what must not be published (placeholders, missing media, broken rules of the widgets); warnings are the principles a designer may break on purpose. A recipe that breaks one declares it in `deviations` with the reason; the internal suite fails on an undeclared warning.
6. **Generated files.** `references/recipes/INDEX.md` and `references/presets/INDEX.md` are the output of `design.py recipes list --markdown` and `design.py presets list --markdown`; `required_defaults` in the preset files is written by the internal suite's generator from the schema snapshots. Regenerate them, do not edit them. The rule table in `references/plans.md` mirrors `designlib/lint.py` `RULES` and is checked against it.

## Where things live

- `scripts/design.py` — the only entry point; `scripts/designlib/` — private package (`catalog` loads references, `apply` merges presets, `lint` checks plans, `workspace` reads a pulled workspace without writing it).
- `references/presets/<Widget>.json` — per widget: summary, rules, `skeleton_fix` (schema defaults the constructor's skeleton can lose), `module_template`, `required_defaults` (the required keys of every object a preset can create, for sparse pulled sections; store values and platform copy as `<fill: …>` markers), presets. A preset's optional `unset` lists dotted keys `presets apply` removes from the base (a merge can only add or overwrite); `doctor` refuses a key that is both written and unset. A widget the sample gives no reason to change has no presets and a `leave_default` advice instead.
- `scripts/designlib/touch.py` — the phone-height estimate the lint uses; its costs follow phone captures of the sample.
- `references/recipes/<id>.json` — one recipe per file: steps with widget, preset, role; a step that is a `slot` lists its other `variants` (each with `when`), the step itself being the sample's median option; theme presets; `deviations`.
- `references/looks.md` — six looks the slot options are chosen by; `scripts/designlib/typeset.py` — title alignment, measure, the theme font and the H1 shared by `presets apply` and the lint; `scripts/designlib/claims.py` — advantages and the free-delivery line as claims.
- `references/*.md` — principles, phones (`mobile.md`), product page, listings, theme, native vs custom, plan format, checklist.
- The evaluation suite for this skill lives in the internal repository, not here.
